## Description

```text
RBX15 Classic Shirt and Pants Maker is a Windows editor for Roblox Classic (R15) shirt
and pants templates. It works on the exact 585x559 template grid, so what you see on the
canvas is what the exported PNG contains.

Compose layers, paint the template's regions with solid colors, gradients and patterns,
draw, add Word Art, then download a PNG ready to upload to Roblox. A 3D preview shows the
template on an avatar and follows every edit.

The app runs on your PC and makes no network requests. There is no account, no telemetry
and no ads. Your projects and exports save where you choose.
```

## Features

```text
- A large canvas that fills the window, built for the exact 585x559 template grid.
- Layers: reorder, duplicate, hide, and set position, scale, rotation, opacity and flips.
- Non-destructive adjustments: brightness, contrast, saturation, hue and per-channel
  color shift.
- Region fills: solid, linear or radial gradient, stripes, checker, dots or camo, with
  patterns and gradients flowing across connected panels.
- Word Art: 15 fonts and six styles (solid, gradient, neon, shadow, outline, retro).
- Drawing tools: pen, line, rectangle, ellipse and eraser.
- Clothing details: collars, cuffs, hems, seams, pockets and plackets.
- Bundled Drips and Word Art packs, or open any folder of images.
- A 3D preview on a Classic or R15 avatar.
- Undo and redo that cover every edit.
- Autosave and crash recovery, a recent projects list, a taskbar jump list, and open from
  Explorer.
- Paste an image from the clipboard as a new layer.
- Works offline. Fonts are bundled.
```

## What's new in this version

```text
RBX15 5.0 is rebuilt as a native Windows app. Your .r15proj projects open as they are.

Quit RBX15 before updating. Save your work and close the app first, so the update can
replace its files.

- Native and smaller. The package is about 69 MB, down from 141 MB.
- Works offline. The app makes no network requests. Word Art and interface fonts are
  bundled and look the same without a connection.
- 3D preview. A panel beside the canvas shows your template on an avatar and follows
  every edit. Drag to turn it.
- Autosave and recovery. Unsaved work is copied every 60 seconds. After a crash, the next
  launch offers to restore it. Your own file is never overwritten.
- Recent projects. The last 10 are in a Recent button beside Open and on the taskbar jump
  list.
- Open from Explorer. Double-click a .r15proj file. Each project opens in its own window.
- Paste. Ctrl+V adds the clipboard image as a new layer.
- Undo covers every edit, with redo on Ctrl+Y.
- Keyboard focus shows a cyan ring, and controls have names for Narrator.

Known limits: high-contrast themes are not supported, and edits made on the canvas are
not announced by screen readers.

Independent third-party tool. "Roblox" is a trademark of Roblox Corporation,
used nominatively to describe the template file format. RBX15 Classic Shirt and
Pants Maker is not affiliated with or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.
```
