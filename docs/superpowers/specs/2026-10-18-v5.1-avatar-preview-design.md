# v5.1 3D avatar preview: design

Status: approved for an unattended Cart build, 2026-10-18 (Este: "get the 3D model mocked up and spec'd out and built").
Dashboard task: `task-1792281341358-l9vnpmly7`. Run: `docs/cart-runs/2026-10-18-avatar-preview-3d/`.

## What it is

A live 3D preview of the open template on Roblox's classic blocky avatar, in a panel to the right of the canvas. It
turns slowly on its own, the user can drag it to any angle, and it updates as the document changes. Shirt mode dresses
the torso and both arms; pants mode dresses the torso and both legs. Everything not dressed is the plain body color.

New in v5 (4.1 has no 3D view). It adds one ledger row, `avatar-preview-3d`, disposition `new`.

## Out of scope

- R15 and Rthro bodies. Roblox splits the limbs and stretches the texture there. The panel says so in one line.
- A shirt and a pants document shown together. v5 edits one document at a time.
- Accessories, faces, animation, body colors chosen by the user, and saving the preview as an image.
- Persisting the panel's visibility across launches (the settings store has no key for it; session-only for now).

## Coordinates (studs)

WPF's right-handed space, Y up. The avatar faces +Z, toward a camera on +Z looking at -Z. So the character's right is
-X (the viewer's left when the avatar faces them), the same way the template draws the torso's right side to the left
of its front.

| Part | Template slot | X | Y | Z |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Torso | `BodyPart.Body` | -1 .. 1 | 2 .. 4 | -0.5 .. 0.5 |
| Right arm (shirt) | `BodyPart.RightLimb` | -2 .. -1 | 2 .. 4 | -0.5 .. 0.5 |
| Left arm (shirt) | `BodyPart.LeftLimb` | 1 .. 2 | 2 .. 4 | -0.5 .. 0.5 |
| Right leg (pants) | `BodyPart.RightLimb` | -1 .. 0 | 0 .. 2 | -0.5 .. 0.5 |
| Left leg (pants) | `BodyPart.LeftLimb` | 0 .. 1 | 0 .. 2 | -0.5 .. 0.5 |
| Head | none, body color | -0.6 .. 0.6 | 4 .. 5.2 | -0.6 .. 0.6 |

Torso faces are 2x2 (front, back), 1x2 (sides) and 2x1 (up, down); the template regions are 128x128, 64x128 and 128x64,
so every face is 64 template pixels per stud. Limbs are 1x2 with 64x128 sides and 64x64 caps.

## Face orientation (the one table every box uses)

The template is an unfolded box: each region's neighbor on the image is its neighbor on the body. Each face is seen
from outside the box, and its four corners map to the region's corners in this order: top-left, top-right,
bottom-right, bottom-left. For a box `[x0,x1] x [y0,y1] x [z0,z1]`:

| Face | Normal | TL | TR | BR | BL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Front | +Z | (x0,y1,z1) | (x1,y1,z1) | (x1,y0,z1) | (x0,y0,z1) |
| Back | -Z | (x1,y1,z0) | (x0,y1,z0) | (x0,y0,z0) | (x1,y0,z0) |
| Right (character's right) | -X | (x0,y1,z0) | (x0,y1,z1) | (x0,y0,z1) | (x0,y0,z0) |
| Left (character's left) | +X | (x1,y1,z1) | (x1,y1,z0) | (x1,y0,z0) | (x1,y0,z1) |
| Up | +Y | (x0,y1,z0) | (x1,y1,z0) | (x1,y1,z1) | (x0,y1,z1) |
| Down | -Y | (x0,y0,z1) | (x1,y0,z1) | (x1,y0,z0) | (x0,y0,z0) |

Checks that hold for every box (and that the engine tests pin):

- Front's left edge is Right's right edge; Front's right edge is Left's left edge; Up's bottom edge is Front's top
  edge; Down's top edge is Front's bottom edge.
- Torso strip `Right | Front | Left | Back` (x 165, 231, 361, 427) and the limb strips `L B R F` (right limb) and
  `F L B R` (left limb) each wrap with shared edges between image neighbors.
- TL, TR, BR, BL runs clockwise seen from outside, so the two triangles are indices `0,3,2` and `0,2,1` (TL, BL, BR
  and TL, BR, TR): counter-clockwise from outside, which is WPF's front face, so each normal points out.

Texture coordinates are the region's corners in template pixels, inset by 0.5 px on every side, so bilinear sampling
never reaches the 2 px gaps between regions.

## The texture

`Renderer.Export(doc)` (the masked image Roblox receives) drawn over a fill of the body color, at 585x559, opaque. A
transparent pixel on the shirt shows the body through, as on Roblox. Parts the mode does not dress use the body color
alone. Body color: Roblox "Medium stone grey", `#A3A2A5`.

## Engine (WPF-free, tested headless)

`src/RBX15.Engine/Avatar.cs`:

- `AvatarPart { Head, Torso, RightArm, LeftArm, RightLeg, LeftLeg }`.
- `AvatarBox(AvatarPart Part, Vec3 Min, Vec3 Max, IReadOnlyList<AvatarFace> Faces, bool Dressed)`, with
  `AvatarFace(Face Face, Region? Region, Vec3[4] Corners, (float U, float V)[4] Uv)`: corners TL, TR, BR, BL, UV in
  template pixels (inset), Region null and UV empty on an undressed box.
- `Avatar.Build(TemplateMode mode)`: the six boxes. Torso and the mode's two limbs are dressed through
  `Template.Slot(mode, part, face)`; the other two limbs and the head are not.
- `Avatar.BodyColor` and `Avatar.RenderTexture(Document doc)`: the opaque 585x559 texture. The caller disposes it.
- `Vec3` is a small `readonly record struct Vec3(double X, double Y, double Z)`; the engine takes no WPF dependency.

## App

- `AvatarPreviewViewModel` over the `EditorSession`: `Mode`, `Boxes` (from `Avatar.Build`), the texture as a frozen
  `BitmapSource`, `Yaw` and `Pitch` (degrees; pitch clamped to -30 .. 30), `IsVisible`. A document change marks the
  texture stale and schedules one rebuild at `DispatcherPriority.Background`, so a drag's many changes cost one render
  per idle moment. A mode change rebuilds the boxes too. Nothing renders while the panel is hidden; showing it
  rebuilds once.
- `Views/AvatarPreviewPanel.xaml` (+ `.cs`): a `Viewport3D` with one `GeometryModel3D` per box (a `MeshGeometry3D`
  with 4 positions and 2 triangles per face; dressed boxes use an `ImageBrush` with `ViewportUnits="Absolute"` and
  `Viewport="0,0,1,1"`, texture coordinates normalized by 585 and 559; undressed boxes a body-color brush), an
  `AmbientLight` and one `DirectionalLight` from the front, upper left. A `PerspectiveCamera` frames the whole body.
  Rotation is yaw about Y, then pitch about X, through the body's center.
- Interaction: a left drag turns the avatar (0.5 degrees per DIP horizontally for yaw, vertically for pitch);
  double-click returns to the front. The idle spin turns 20 degrees a second while the pointer is not dragging, pauses
  on press, and resumes 2 s after release. It is off when `SystemParameters.ClientAreaAnimation` is false (Windows
  "Show animations" off) and while the panel is hidden or the window minimized.
- Panel: a third column in `AppRow`, 260 DIPs (200 at the 900 DIP breakpoint), styled like the left panel (navy-deep
  to ink-900, 1px border-1 left, 14 padding), heading "3D Preview" with `PanelHeading`, the viewport, and one fg-3 note:
  "Classic body. R15 and Rthro avatars stretch the texture." Automation name "3D preview"; the viewport is
  focusable, and Left / Right arrows turn it 15 degrees, Home returns to the front.
- Toggle: a "3D preview" checkbox in the Home ribbon's Settings group, on by default. Session state only: not in the
  document, not an undo step, not saved. Off collapses the column to 0 and stops all rendering.

## Testing

- Engine: geometry, sizes, the face table, the shared-edge checks, outward winding, UV insets and per-mode dressing
  (shirt dresses arms, pants dresses legs, both dress the torso, the head never). `RenderTexture` is opaque, body
  color in a gap pixel and in an empty region, and the export's pixel inside a filled region.
- App: the view-model coalesces many document changes into one rebuild, rebuilds on mode change, does nothing while
  hidden. A view test renders the panel's `Viewport3D` through `RenderTargetBitmap` at a fixed size and reads
  pixels away from edges: torso front filled red at yaw 0 reads red-dominant at the torso's center; torso back
  filled blue reads blue-dominant at yaw 180; in shirt mode a green right-arm front shows green on the viewer's left
  of the torso, and in pants mode the legs carry the texture while the arms are body color. No test asserts an exact
  pixel position (display-scale independent).
- Ledger: the `avatar-preview-3d` row (`App.Shell`, `ui-auto`, `new`, done when the tests pass), Markdown
  re-rendered with `python tools/ledger/ledger.py render`, `ledger.py check` clean.
